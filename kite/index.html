Frequently asked questions

Everything people search before opening their first demat account.

Is opening a Zerodha account really free?

Yes — online account opening is free. There is a ₹300/year demat maintenance charge (AMC), which is standard across brokers, and your signup reward points can be used to offset it.

What do I get for signing up through the referral link?

You receive 300 reward points, which can be used toward AMC or Zerodha's partner products like Sensibull (options), Streak (algo trading) and Smallcase. The person who referred you earns a small share of brokerage — it costs you nothing extra.

What documents do I need?

PAN card, Aadhaar linked to your mobile number, bank proof (cancelled cheque or statement), and your signature. Income proof (bank statement/salary slip) is needed only for F&O activation.

How long does account opening take?

The online form takes about 15 minutes. Approval and activation usually happen within 24–48 working hours, after which you get your Client ID by email.

Is my money and are my shares safe with Zerodha?

Zerodha is SEBI-registered and a member of NSE, BSE and MCX. Your shares are held in your own demat account with CDSL (a government-regulated depository) — not with the broker. Funds are in your linked bank account or your trading balance, which is reported to exchanges.

Can I invest in mutual funds and IPOs too?

Yes — direct mutual funds with zero commission through Coin, and IPO applications directly from Console/Kite using UPI.

Zerodha vs bank demat account — which is better?

Bank brokers typically charge 0.3–0.5% brokerage on delivery (₹300–500 per ₹1 lakh). Zerodha charges ₹0. For an investor, that difference compounds into a large amount over years.

Do I need money to open the account?

No. You can open the account with zero balance and add funds only when you're ready to invest. There's no minimum balance requirement.
